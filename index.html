Our Story

From Local Bakery to Certified Manufacturer

Jang‑Jang Bakeshop began as a humble neighborhood bakery known for its soft breads and heartwarming pastries. Over time, our passion for quality and consistency led us to expand into commercial manufacturing. Whether you’re buying wholesale or enjoying a fresh loaf from our shop, Jang‑Jang Bakeshop stands for one promise — every product is baked with love and made to last.
