Standard Operating Procedures

Transparency and consistency are the cornerstones of our operations. Below is a public overview of the SOPs governing our manufacturing, quality, and warehouse processes.

Note: Full detailed SOP manuals are available for regulatory audit and B2B partner review upon request.

🍞 Production Operations

SOP-PRO-001: Production & Batch Record Management

Standardizes the documentation of every production batch, ensuring traceability from raw materials to finished goods.

SOP-PRO-002: Biscocho Production & CCP Oversight

Defines the critical control points (CCPs) for temperature and moisture during the Biscocho baking and toasting processes.

SOP-PRO-003: Otap Production & CCP Oversight

Outlines the lamination, baking, and cooling parameters required to achieve the signature flaky texture of Otap while ensuring food safety.

SOP-PAC-001: Packaging Operations

Governs the pre-coding, filling, sealing, and boxing procedures to prevent contamination and ensure product integrity.

🔬 Quality Assurance & Food Safety

SOP-QC-001: In-Process Quality Control

Establishes checkpoints during manufacturing (e.g., dough temperature, weight verification) to catch deviations early.

SOP-QC-002: Finished Product Inspection & Release

Defines the criteria for organoleptic testing, label verification, and final batch release by the Food Safety Compliance Officer.

SOP-QC-004: Non-Conforming Product Control

Procedures for quarantining, evaluating, and disposing of or reworking products that fail quality standards.

SOP-LAB-001: Product Labeling and Traceability

Ensures all products carry accurate FDA-compliant labels, including allergen declarations, lot codes, and expiry dates.

📦 Warehouse & Materials Management

SOP-REC-001: Receiving & Acceptance of Raw Materials

Details the inspection protocol for incoming deliveries, including temperature checks for perishables and verification of supplier CoAs.

SOP-WAR-001: Warehouse Inventory & FEFO Rotation

Enforces the First Expired, First Out (FEFO) system to prevent the use of expired ingredients and ensure proper stock rotation.

⚙️ Maintenance & Sanitation

SOP-MNT-002: Calibration Control

Schedules and manages the calibration of all measuring devices (scales, thermometers) to ensure accuracy in production.

SOP-SAN-001: Sanitation & GMP Compliance

Outlines daily cleaning schedules, pest control monitoring, and personnel hygiene requirements (gowning, handwashing).

For a complete copy of our Quality Manual or to schedule a facility audit, please contact our Compliance Team.
