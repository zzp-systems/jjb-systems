Equipment Calibration & Verification

All critical measuring and monitoring devices are subject to a rigorous calibration schedule to ensure accuracy in production and quality control.

  • Weighing Scales: Annual third-party calibration with official NMI stamp.
  • Thermometers: Quarterly ice-point and boiling-point verification for CCP monitoring.
  • Oven Sensors: Annual third-party calibration for temperature uniformity.

✅ Calibration certificates are maintained and available for regulatory inspection.
